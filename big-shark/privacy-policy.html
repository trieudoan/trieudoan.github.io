Privacy Policy for Big Shark

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use the mobile game Big Shark ("the Game").

1. Information We Collect

The Game is developed using the Unity engine. By playing this game, certain non-personal data may be collected to help us improve the gaming experience and analyze performance. This includes:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and hardware identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Gameplay statistics, achievements, and progress data.

2. Third-Party Services

We utilize the following third-party services which may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • AdMob: Used to serve advertisements within the Game. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers in accordance with their AdMob Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used to manage game achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save functionality. Google collects data in accordance with their Google Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • Serve and manage advertisements through AdMob.
  • Manage user progress, leaderboards, and achievements via Google Play Services.
  • Analyze game performance and identify areas for improvement.

4. Data Retention

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Data related to your progress (such as achievements and scores) is handled by the Google Play Services platform.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at trieudoanit@gmail.com.
