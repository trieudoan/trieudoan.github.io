BIG SHARK 2 / WINDOWS DESKTOP EDITION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes data handling in the Windows desktop edition of Big Shark 2, developed by trieudoanit ("we" or "us"). It does not describe the Android edition, which has a separate privacy policy, or replace the policies of a store, website or email service you use.

The Windows game saves progress and settings on your device. It does not require a game account and has no in-game purchases, advertisements, online leaderboards or cloud-save service.

1. Local gameplay data

To remember your progress and preferences, the game stores:

  • Campaign progress, shark unlocks, selected shark, scores, play times and recent run history.
  • Shark Mastery, Daily Hunt, Collection and local achievement progress.
  • Music, sound and graphics preferences.
  • Dates, progress counters and randomly generated local identifiers used to keep progression records consistent.

These records are used by the game's local progression features. Those features do not upload your saves or scores to us or publish them to other players. The Windows edition does not connect to Google Play Games or Amazon purchasing services.

2. Unity engine and diagnostic information

The game is built with Unity. The game and its runtime can process technical information needed to run and diagnose it, such as the operating system, graphics hardware, driver details and error messages. Local player logs and crash files may also include local file paths.

Unity Analytics and cloud crash reporting services are not configured for this Windows edition, and its project is not connected to Unity Cloud. Local logs are separate from your gameplay save data. We do not provide an automatic support-report upload feature in the game.

Unity's privacy information is available in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Diagnostic handling by Windows or a store is governed by that provider's settings and policies.

3. Stores and payments

Obtaining the game through a store is separate from playing it. The store and its payment providers handle information you give them, such as account and payment details, under their own policies. The Windows game does not ask for payment-card details or process shark purchases.

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement, including Microsoft Store and Windows services

Store accounts, purchase records, downloads, reviews and any store-provided backups are managed separately from the game's local saves.

If a storefront makes customer or purchase information available to us, we use it only for customer support and accounting.

4. Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, such as screenshots, logs or save files. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Email is handled by our email service provider.

Only send information needed for your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unrelated personal information. You can ask us about access to, correction of or deletion of information you have provided.

5. Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed. Uninstalling the game may leave some saves, preferences, logs or backups behind. Backups made by Windows, a store or your own backup software may retain additional copies.

You can remove local game data using Windows file or app-data controls. Close the game first and keep a backup if you want to retain progress: deleting saves can permanently remove unlocks and records. Contact us if you need help locating the files for your installation.

Removing local saves does not remove store purchase records or messages already sent to us. Contact the relevant store for its records, or email us about support correspondence. We retain support information while needed to handle the request and any related recordkeeping obligations.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Windows game's features or data handling change. The revised policy and its updated date will appear on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact trieudoanit@gmail.com. Please mention Big Shark 2 for Windows and your game version.
